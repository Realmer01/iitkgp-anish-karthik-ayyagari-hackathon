# Model card - RiskPulse risk engine

Written in the spirit of model-risk management practice (conceptual soundness, outcome analysis, ongoing
monitoring). Current figures for every metric named here are in `reports/metrics.json`
(regenerate with `python run.py evaluate`).

## Intended use

Triage of financial news and social posts for a risk desk: flag which items matter, for which company,
sector or market, and how severely; feed an index-tilting tool and an event-driven stress test.
**Not intended** as a trading signal, a credit-rating input, or a regulatory capital calculation.

## Components

| Component | Method | Training data | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entity linker | Cashtags, exchange prefixes, unambiguous tickers, alias and case-sensitive alias dictionaries, broker-context and false-positive guards | `config/universe.yaml` | company / sector / MARKET entities |
| Sentiment (full) | FinBERT (ProsusAI/finbert) blended with a logistic-regression head on bge-small embeddings, weight per source type (news 0.8 FinBERT, social 0.1); monetary-policy polarity rule on Macroeconomic events | head: 9.5k labelled finance tweets (train split) | score in [-1, 1], label, confidence |
| Sentiment (lite) | VADER plus a finance lexicon; neutral band tuned on a dev split of the training tweets | same | same |
| Event classifier | Logistic regression on bge-small embeddings (full) or TF-IDF (lite), ensembled with high-precision rules | 15.4k finance tweets weakly labelled into 12 risk classes + 85 synthetic seed headlines (fictional companies) | event type, confidence, class probabilities, rule fired |
| Impact score | `1 + 9·σ(b + Σ wᵢfᵢ)` over 9 features in [0, 1]; expert weights (`config/impact.yaml`), intercept rescaled so ~10% of signals exceed 7 | weights set by judgement, checked by event study | score 1-10 with per-feature contributions |

## Validation (outcome analysis)

| Question | Test | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Is sentiment right on text no model saw? | Accuracy and macro-F1 on the tweet validation split and on 400 real news headlines labelled blind to model output, against naive VADER | `metrics.sentiment` |
| Are events right? | Accuracy, macro-F1 and per-class F1 on the same headlines, against a keyword-only baseline; ablation without rules | `metrics.events` |
| Does impact mean anything? | Market-adjusted abnormal returns around 6,500+ company-days; Spearman correlation, decile means and top-decile hit rate against \|sentiment\| alone and news volume; market-wide signals against the S&P 500's own move | `metrics.impact` |
| Can the weights be improved? | Walk-forward calibration (non-negative logistic weights, train on the first 60% of days, test once on the last 40%); adopted only if it beats the expert weights by > 0.01 out of sample | `metrics.impact.calibration` |
| Do the signals predict returns? | Hourly backtest with strict no-look-ahead, daily cross-sectional IC, placebo (ticker shuffle), +1-day delay test | `metrics.module_a` |

**Findings to keep in mind.** Sentiment and events beat their naive baselines on real headlines. The impact
score is an explanatory severity measure: higher scores coincide with larger moves around the news, and for
market-wide news it ranks moves far better than |sentiment| or news volume. For single-stock moves,
|sentiment| alone ranks slightly better, and event-class priors and company size do not explain percentage
moves. After the news is public, no feature predicts the size or direction of the next move reliably,
consistent with prices of heavily covered mega caps absorbing news quickly.

## Limitations

- About 97% of archive headlines carry a date only, so historical tests use day-level timing.
- 16 mega-cap names and two years of data: small, survivorship-biased sample (current S&P 100 members).
- Gold labels come from one annotator (AI-assisted, blind to model output); small classes are unstable.
- Weak supervision: event training labels are mapped from topic labels, not hand-labelled.
- English only.
- Entity-level sentiment splits sentences and contrastive clauses; it is not a target-aware model.

## Monitoring (production)

| Signal | Trigger for review |
|---|---|
| Sentiment distribution per source (population stability index vs a reference month) | PSI > 0.2 |
| Event-class mix per source | any class share moves by > 50% relative |
| Share of unlinked documents, source error rate | doubles week on week |
| Gold-set re-score (monthly) | macro-F1 drops by > 0.05 |
| Impact event study (quarterly) | Spearman vs S&P moves turns non-positive |
| p95 latency | above 1 s per document |

## Governance

Every signal carries `schema_version`, `model_version`, `model_tier`, the event rule that fired and the
impact drivers, so any decision can be traced to its inputs. All thresholds are configuration
(`config/*.yaml`), all results are regenerated by one command, and the synthetic data generators are seeded.
